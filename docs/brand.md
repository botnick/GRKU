# GRKU brand identity

The short version: what a page, a guide, or a camera screen of GRKU needs to look and sound like. Everything else is taste, decided per case.

## 1. Name and voice

- Brand: GRKU. Thai name: จีอาร์คุ. Website: https://grku.app
- Tagline: GR ของคุณ ในแบบที่ชอบ
- One sentence: a tool for people who love the Ricoh GR, easy to use, Thai first.
- Voice: Thai is the main language. Short sentences, plain words, friendly, no technical terms that a new user does not need. Explain so that a beginner gets it on the first read.
- GRKU is an independent tool for Ricoh GR users and is not affiliated with Ricoh. Say so wherever the brand speaks for the first time: the footer of the site, the README, the guides.

## 2. Logo

- Wordmark: GRKU. — the four letters in Inter Extra Bold, tight letter-spacing, then a period in brand red.
- Two versions: black on white, white on black. Nothing else: no outline, no gradient, no icon beside the word.
- Clear space around it: the height of the letter K on every side. Never smaller than 24 px high on a screen.

## 3. Colors and how to use them

| Color | Hex | Use it for | Never for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Black | #171717 | Text, the wordmark, dark panels (the camera menu panel on the site), primary buttons on hover | Large filled areas behind text, other than the camera menu panel |
| White | #F7F7F2 | The page background, text on black | — |
| Gray | #A3A3A3 | Captions, credits, placeholders, the version mark, dividers at low weight | Body text or anything a person must read (it fails the contrast test on white); body text that matters less uses #6E6E6E |
| Red | #D94135 | One action per screen (Install, Write to card), the period of the wordmark, the focus ring, a selected mark, an error | Headings, body text, decoration, a second button on the same screen |

Rules of thumb:
- A screen has one red thing. If two things are red, one of them is wrong.
- Gray says "you may skip this". Black says "read this".
- Lines are 1 px in #DEDEDA. No shadows, no gradients, no rounded cards: the content sits on the paper.
- The film boxes, the camera tiles, and the photos bring their own colors. Around them the page stays black, white, and gray, so they carry the eye.

## 4. Type

- English and numbers: Inter. Headlines Extra Bold (800) with tight letter-spacing; body Regular (400); labels Medium (500).
- Thai: IBM Plex Sans Thai, the same weights. Thai body text takes a little more line height (1.7).
- Code, file names, versions: JetBrains Mono, Regular.
- Sizes: one headline per screen, large; section titles half its size; body 16 to 18 px; captions 14 to 15 px. Sentence case everywhere, no capitals for emphasis.

## 5. Layout and pictures

- Japanese minimal, Thai friendly: wide margins, few lines, one idea per block, left aligned, line length under 70 characters.
- Show the product: real photos from a GR, the film boxes of the library, the camera's own screens. No stock photos, no illustrations of abstract shapes, no emoji.
- Motion only when a person acts (a slider, a choice, a confirmation). Nothing moves by itself.

## 6. Where the brand shows

- Website grku.app: the wordmark top left, the one red action in the first screen, Thai as the first language with English, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean as choices.
- Camera: the GRKU entry in ADJ, the GRKU section of MENU, About GRKU, and the power-on label "GRKU Loaded".
- Documents: README, installation guides, release notes. The disclaimer in the last paragraph.
