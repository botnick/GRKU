# Most popular photographic film stocks, 2026 — ranked shortlist for 40 default presets

Compiled 2026-11-08 for the RICOH GR film-look add-on (camera holds 40 presets).

## Method and caveats

- Signals weighted in this order: (1) retailer sales signals 2025-2026, (2) community use surveys, (3) digital-emulation / recipe popularity (Fuji X Weekly page-view rankings, Filmbox and Dehancer profile libraries), (4) press coverage volume.
- Retailer front-ends that expose a best-selling sort (Analogue Wonderland, CineStill, Kamerastore, B&H, Yodobashi, Lomography shop) all refused automated fetches (HTTP 403/429), so retailer evidence is second-hand: the KEH Tilt-Shift sales report, B&H "Top Seller" badges as quoted by other sites, Amazon US sales trackers, and the Japanese FilmFocus 2025 survey. Where a ranking rests mostly on general knowledge the evidence cell says so.
- No single 2025/2026 r/analog or r/AnalogCommunity poll was found; a Reddit review-mention aggregator (GummySearch) is used as a weak proxy.
- Kodak names: Eastman Kodak took over distribution/branding from Kodak Alaris starting 1 Oct 2025 (Kodacolor 100/200 first, Gold/UltraMax 3 Nov 2025, Ektar/Tri-X 21 Jan 2026, Ektachrome 11 Feb 2026). On 24 Mar 2026 Portra 160/400/800 became **Ektacolor Pro 160/400/800** and T-Max 100/400/P3200 became **Ektapan 100/400/P3200**; emulsions unchanged ("same film, new boxes"). Ektar, Tri-X, Gold, UltraMax, Ektachrome kept their names. ColorPlus 200 is sold by Eastman as **Kodacolor 200** and Pro Image 100 as **Kodacolor 100** (widely reported equivalence; Kodak has not formally stated it). Old Kodak Alaris boxes remain on shelves until sold through. Presets should carry both names.

## 2026 market changes to encode in preset names and metadata

- Kodak Alaris to Eastman Kodak transition: 1 Oct 2025 start; Kodacolor 100/200 (ex Pro Image 100 / ColorPlus 200) first; Gold and UltraMax 3 Nov 2025; Ektar and Tri-X 21 Jan 2026; Ektachrome 11 Feb 2026; Portra → Ektacolor Pro 160/400/800 and T-Max → Ektapan 100/400/P3200 on 24 Mar 2026. Emulsions unchanged. Reported 2026 prices: Ektacolor Pro 160 35mm $16.99, 800 35mm $19.95; 120 five-packs $64.95 / $89.95. Ektapan P3200 reportedly 135 only. (PetaPixel, PhotoWorkout, PhotographyTalk, Fuji X Weekly, Analog.Cafe.)
- Kodak VERITA 200D 5206/7206: new daylight 200 colour negative motion-picture stock, announced 13 Apr 2026 (65/35/16mm), developed for Euphoria S3; speculation only that it resembles Portra 400; not yet a still-photo product. (Kodak IR, Newsshooter.)
- Fujifilm: Pro 400H discontinued Jan 2021; Pro 160NS Oct 2021; Velvia 50 sheet sizes Oct 2021 (35mm/120 continue but sold out across shops Oct 2026); Velvia 100 discontinued in the US 2021, still listed by Fujifilm Japan; Provia 100F listed but out of stock; Superia X-Tra 400 ended Apr 2024 → Kodak-made Fujifilm 400; C200 → Kodak-made Fujifilm 200; Superia Premium 400 and Fujicolor 100 are Japan-only with short-roll variants stopped and a discontinuation rumour denied Jan 2026; Acros II readily available; Instax capacity +20% from 2028. Japan survey 2025: Kodak 71% vs Fujifilm 68% brand reach (81% vs 84% in 2024), Ilford 41%, Lomography 30%; colour negative 82%, B&W 61%. (Pellica, PhotoWorkout/PetaPixel, Photrio.)
- Harman: Phoenix II 200 launched 16 Jul 2025 (35mm/120), broadly well received; **Switch Azure** (ISO 125, colour-shift negative, compared to LomoChrome Turquoise) launched Mar 2026 after a Feb leak, teased as a 5 Mar announcement. Too new/niche to rank. (Harman site, Analog.Cafe, DIYPhotography.)
- Lomography: LomoChrome Classicolor 200 (2025; now preloaded in Simple Use cameras incl. a half-frame model from Jul 2026), LomoChrome Color '92 "Sun-Kissed" (2026, possibly Wolfen NC500-based), and a "f2/400" colour negative reported Mar 2026 (forum consensus leans Kodak-made; unconfirmed). None yet rank. (RedShark, PetaPixel, Photrio, Imaging Resource.)
- CineStill: 800T and 400D remain the brand's core; no new stock found in 2025-2026. Store ranking page blocked.
- Reflx Lab: two new 110 stocks (Jul 2026); acts as Lucky distributor. Lucky Color 400 pre-release May 2026, expected mid-2026; Lucky C400 early batches had colour-cast issues. Shanghai GP3 remains a niche B&W. Film Washi: no 2025-2026 coverage found. (PetaPixel, 35mmc, Analog.Cafe.)
- Orwo/Wolfen: NC500 (2022) and NC400 (2023) got lukewarm reviews; NC200/OptiColour 200 (2025, also sold as KONO Color 200) scans better; InovisCoat and FilmoTec were acquired by Optik Oldschool in Jul 2026 and the ORWO brand has decoupled from manufacturing. Low popularity; exclude from defaults. (PetaPixel, Analog.Cafe, 35mmc, Analog Reference.)
- Prices: Analog.Cafe Film Price Watch tracked 37 stocks across 13 stores; +2.5% Jan-Jul 2026 after 0% in the prior six months; biggest risers ColorPlus/Kodacolor 200 (+10.5%), XP2 Super, FP4 Plus. (PetaPixel, Analog.Cafe.)
