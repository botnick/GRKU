The film-look search on the install page and the Develop page ignores case, spaces, and punctuation; matches the brand, the name and number on the box, the ID, and the kind; knows other names in Latin letters and Thai (fuji, ฟูจิ, โกดัก, พอร์ตร้า, ขาวดำ, b&w, ฟายน์พิกซ์, …); forgives a typo or two in a long word; and puts the exact name first, then names that start with the search, then other names, then near misses. A search that finds nothing names what was searched, offers the closest looks (or the popular ones), Clear search and Show all, and links to make the look from a LUT or a Lightroom preset or to ask for it. The count reads "N looks", and Select all hides when nothing matches. The kinds of film on a phone no longer overlap the count.
