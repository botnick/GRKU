The assistant takes photos: send a picture of the camera screen or of the files on the card (from the camera or the library on a phone, or paste or drop it on a computer), and it reads the screen, says what it shows, and gives the steps. The photo is shrunk and stripped of its metadata in your browser, goes to the AI only for the answer, and is never stored. Photos have their own daily limit.
