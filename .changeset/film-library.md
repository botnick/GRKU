The film library has 104 looks: 83 new ones fitted from the film emulation presets of Pat David (CC BY-SA 4.0) and from t3mujinpack (MIT), with Ilford, Agfa, Polaroid, Fuji instant, Lomography, and Rollei films, the Kodak and Fuji slide and negative films that were missing, and black-and-white films. A package carries 40 of them; `GRKU-film-library.zip` has every effect file to copy into `GRKIT/EFFECTS` on the card.
