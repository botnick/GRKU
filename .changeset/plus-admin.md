GRKU Plus has a private admin page for the owner at /admin on the Plus service, behind one password: sales today, in 7 and 30 days with a daily chart and the counts by tier, the recent orders with a status filter, a customer search by email, key, payer name, or order id with the key, what the customer owns, the orders, Copy key, Copy a ready Thai message with the key and the steps, and Email the key again; the key emails log whether the mail server took each one (no content); and the AI chat's counters with its switch and daily limits. The sign-in allows five tries per device in 15 minutes and closes for an hour after 20 wrong tries from anyone.
