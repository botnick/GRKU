GRKU Plus has a private admin page for the owner at /admin on the Plus service, behind one password: sales today, in 7 and 30 days with a daily chart and the counts by tier, the recent orders with a status filter, a customer search by email, key, payer name, or order id with the key, what the customer owns, the orders, Copy key, Copy a ready Thai message with the key and the steps, and Email the key again; the key emails log whether the mail server took each one (no content); and the AI chat: messages, sessions, and tokens of the day against the caps, each provider working, rate-limited, or failing with its last error, each model against its daily cap, the paid models with their spend this month and today in US dollars against the budgets, refusals and blocked messages, with switches for the chat and the paid models, who may use paid models, the budgets, and the daily limits, which take effect within half a minute. The sign-in allows five tries per device in 15 minutes and closes for an hour after 20 wrong tries from anyone.
