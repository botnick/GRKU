Every Film Look icon is now a film box in the colors of the real box, with the brand band, the name, and the speed in Roboto Condensed, and the menu tiles are small color pictures: a film canister for Film Look, a landscape cropped to each ratio for Framing, the guide lines for Guides, an orange date stamp for Date Imprint, a lamp at night for Glow, a grainy picture for Grain, and GRK on a strip of color film in the ADJ toolbar. Each box icon carries a film type stripe (orange negative, blue slide, gray black-and-white, teal motion picture, rose instant). The library has 135 looks: GR2 Positive from the Imprint repository (Apache-2.0), Positive GRII, a look of GRKit itself after the Positive Film setting of the GR II, and 15 new ones from the Fotufilm film profiles (CC BY-SA 4.0), with CineStill 800T and 400D, Harman Phoenix 200, the LomoChrome films, instax, Agfachrome, Kodacolor 100, Pro 160NS, and F-64D. The 40 effects of a package follow the 2026 popularity research in `docs/popular-films-2026.md`.
